About

Meet Sophie Ren

An experienced science educator with a passion for helping students build confidence, curiosity, and strong exam results.

I am an experienced science teacher who has worked in leading independent schools in both Sydney and London. I specialise in teaching IGCSE Biology, GCSE Biology, A Level Biology, and KS3 Science, and have also taught the Australian HSC Biology curriculum.


I graduated from the University of New South Wales (UNSW) with a Bachelor of Secondary Education & Science (Biology major) with Distinction. My teaching is grounded in clear explanations, structured practice, and evidence‑based learning strategies.


I previously worked with Arludo, a science outreach company, designing curriculum that integrates game‑based STEM learning to enrich student engagement in science and biology. This experience has shaped my approach to make lessons interactive, relevant, and enjoyable.

  • • Exam‑focused strategies and past paper practice
  • • Personalised support tailored to each student’s goals
  • • A calm, encouraging environment that builds confidence

Online Tutoring

How Online Lessons Work

All tutoring sessions are delivered online, designed to be interactive, flexible, and effective for busy students.

Lesson Format

  • • Live video lessons (e.g. Zoom or Teams)
  • • Digital whiteboard and shared resources
  • • Exam‑style questions and worked solutions
  • • Optional homework and follow‑up tasks

Benefits of Online

  • • No travel time, learn from home
  • • Flexible scheduling across time zones
  • • Access to digital worksheets and revision materials
  • • Ideal for regular support or intensive revision blocks

Group Sessions

  • • Small groups (typically 2–4 students)
  • • Group discounts offered
  • • Great for friends in the same course
  • • Shared exam practice and discussion
