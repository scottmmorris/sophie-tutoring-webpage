Rates & Enquiries

Tutoring Rates

Rates vary depending on course level, individual vs group sessions, and frequency. Please get in touch for a personalised quote.

What affects the rate?

  • • Course level (KS3, IGCSE/GCSE, A Level)
  • • Individual vs small group lessons
  • • Weekly ongoing support vs short‑term revision
  • • Length of each session

Group discounts are available for students in the same course and similar level.
